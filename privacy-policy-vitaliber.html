Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 11, 2026

NyoarGames ("we", "us", or "our") operates the VitaLiber mobile application (the "App"), available on Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

By using VitaLiber, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — provided directly or through Google Sign-In
  • Authentication credentials — managed securely by Firebase Authentication
  • Display name — if provided through Google Sign-In

1.2 User-Generated Content

Data you create within the App is stored locally on your device:

  • Book collections and custom bookshelves
  • Reading progress, page counts, and read dates
  • Ratings (overall, spice, humor, emotional impact, scare factor, violence, world building, suspense)
  • Written book reviews
  • Reading statistics and history

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information — device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (for analytics and ads)
  • Usage data — app interactions, feature usage, and crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers — used by Google AdMob to serve ads

1.4 Camera and Image Data

If you use the barcode scanning or text recognition features, the App accesses your device camera temporarily to scan book ISBNs or extract text. Images are processed on-device using Google ML Kit and are not transmitted to external servers or stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used

Providing core App features (book tracking, statistics, reviews) User-generated content

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: Your book data, reviews, ratings, and reading progress are stored locally on your device using an SQLite database. This data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Authentication data: Managed by Firebase Authentication with industry-standard encryption.
  • Subscription data: Managed by RevenueCat and the respective app store (Google Play or Apple App Store).
  • CSV exports: When you export your data, the CSV file is saved to your device locally. We do not have access to exported files.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With third-party service providers listed above, solely for the purposes described
  • For advertising — anonymized device identifiers may be shared with ad networks through Google AdMob
  • As required by law — to comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms, or protect rights and safety

6. Your Rights and Choices

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). This permanently removes your Firebase authentication data. Locally stored book data is removed when you uninstall the App.

Data Export

You can export all your book data as a CSV file at any time from within the App, giving you full control over your data.

Ad Personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

Additional Rights (where applicable)

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have additional rights including:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

VitaLiber is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your authentication data is removed from Firebase. Locally stored data remains on your device until the App is uninstalled. Ad-related data is retained according to Google AdMob's policies.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own through our third-party service providers (primarily Google and RevenueCat, based in the United States). These providers maintain appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
